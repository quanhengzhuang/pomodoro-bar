# Refresh Photo Timeline Colors

## Goal

Give Photo Timeline a brighter, younger travel-journal feel while keeping photos and time information clear.

## Changes

- Use vivid indigo `#5546DB` for date circles, times, and interactive information; use berry `#D3326A` for primary actions.
- Use pale violet-white `#F8F7FF` for the background, soft violet `#EEEAFE` for secondary surfaces, and deep blue-black `#202442` for body text.
- Keep intervals over two hours in an amber-yellow family and intervals under twenty minutes in a green family, adjusting both for contrast.
- Apply the palette across the trip list, timeline, and editor entry points without changing layout, type, or photo crops.
